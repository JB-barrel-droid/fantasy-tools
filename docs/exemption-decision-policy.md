## Scope ladder and decision owners

| Rung | Scope and evidence required | Decision owner |
|---|---|---|
| 0: no exemption | Unknown cause, implementation defect, mismatched shape, or missing evidence. Reproduce and repair; retain the failing gate. | Lane owner diagnoses; Roman coordinates publication repair. Methodology changes go to Jeremy. |
| 1: mechanism-scoped (default) | One named assertion and a pre-described causal channel, with reproducible inputs, bounded effect/count, explicit included and excluded cases, and controls proving unrelated failures remain blocking. Eligibility follows the mechanism across players, scorings, sources, and bakes only within its recorded signature. | A lane owner may apply an already described and authorized shape when all conditions match. A newly discovered shape is not self-authorizing: take it to Jeremy for its initial decision. |
| 2: broader bounded scope | Widen positions, effect/count limits, assertions, or other eligibility beyond the recorded mechanism. Supply multi-setting and multi-bake evidence that the narrower rule excludes genuine designed behavior, identify newly covered risks, explain why a smaller scope cannot work, and specify replacement checks, monitoring, expiry, and rollback. | Jeremy explicitly decides the exact expansion. Neither urgency nor a lane owner's diagnosis supplies approval. |
| 3: change a check's blocking role | Move a class to reporting-only or otherwise remove a publication gate. Requires a written comparison with rung 1/2, retained blocking invariants, a reporting path that actually runs, a repair owner, and a restoration condition. This is a release-policy change, not an ordinary matching exemption. | Jeremy explicitly decides; Roman integrates through the standing gates. |

A request must state its highest rung at the outset. If the evidence already warrants a broader decision, present that option and recommendation once; do not first merge enumerated players and then chase each newly failing bake. Repeated failures alone do not prove designed behavior. An effect or count outside a signature requires a fresh decision, not an automatic threshold increase.

Never exempt canonical identity correctness or unresolved/ambiguous identity, missing-as-zero fabrication, native/provenance falsification, source-vintage relabeling, unauthorized fixture promotion, or an engine/reference disagreement being concealed as a designed effect. A declared source-specific warning rule is distinct from pretending stale data is current. Never exempt the independent-review and CI requirements below. A pricing-methodology repair requires Jeremy's separate authorization; an exemption cannot silently change value math.

## Record, implement, and retire

Record each decision in three linked places:

1. **Linear incident/decision ticket:** decider, timestamp, highest rung, reason, precise predicate, affected assertion, input/bake hashes or immutable versions, reproduction command, full observed effects/counts, counterexamples, replacement controls, repair owner/ticket, expiry or restoration trigger, and rollback. Link JEG-1518 as the governing policy.
2. **Publication runbook/registry:** append the authorized mechanism signature and decision-ticket link alongside this policy; link it from `docs/week-transition-runbook.md`. A lane applying an unchanged signature records the incident instance on its ticket; it need not rewrite the standing rule every week.
3. **Code at the predicate or test-routing change:** comment with ticket, authorization, bounds, causal explanation, logging, and removal trigger. Add positive and negative controls; preserve the diagnostic check and report each use with setting, source, position, player key, delta, effect, and run/head identity.

The PR links all three and records review/CI evidence. A new bake must be checked against the same predicate; a different head invalidates earlier review. On expiry, signature mismatch, or missing evidence, stop treating the case as exempt and return to the decision owner. On repair, remove temporary predicates and restore any reporting-only check to its blocking role, with regression evidence.

## Standing gates remain mandatory

Every assertion-weakening diff needs independent review of the **exact proposed head** and green CI on that head **before merge**. This includes test exclusions, bounds/count changes, skip logic, workflow or Makefile routing, reporting-only moves, and changes that hide a failure without editing an assertion. The author or lane owner cannot independently review their own change. Roman verifies the head, evidence, and applicable merge tier; Jeremy's scope approval does not replace technical review or CI.

Rebase or any further commit requires renewed exact-head review and CI. Rate-limited credentials or unavailable CI mean the gate is unconfirmed: restore headroom/access and obtain the result. “DATA NEVER LATE” makes a held week an urgent same-hour repair; it does not authorize merging without those gates. After integration, rerun the publication chain and verify served week/vintage and rendered output using the publication runbooks.
