| Id | What | Effect | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| G1 | The breakout-by-week series stores rates without cohort sizes or breakout counts. | No sample count or confidence interval for the option-value decay curve. | post 1 research script (JEG-544 lane) |
| G2 | tools/research/bench_value_by_week.py and breakout_rate.py score breakouts with half_ppr, falling back to std, then 0, though the actuals file has a ppr column and the by-week artifact is labelled ppr. | The scoring of the option-value series differs from its label, and a blank score is read as 0. | post 1 research script (JEG-544 lane) |
| G3 | A breakout is defined by full-season points per game, which includes the weeks before the measure week. | A player who scores like a starter for the weeks left but not for the season is not counted, so part of the late fall comes from the target definition, not runway alone. | post 1 research script (JEG-544 lane) |
| G4 | weeks_remaining in the breakout series is 17 minus the measure week for every season, though 2021-2025 have 18 weeks. | The weeks-left axis is approximate for half the seasons. | post 1 research script (JEG-544 lane) |
| G5 | The breakout cohort is every non-starter with a game log, not rostered bench players. | The by-week rates (1-6%) are not on the same scale as the by-depth rates (2-27%). | post 1 research script (JEG-544 lane) |
| G6 | No week-ahead projections are stored by lead time; the JEG-943 backtest compares rest-of-season projections with single weeks over 5 weeks. | No measured basis for a per-week discount (JEG-528 item 2); its Done-when is unmet. | JEG-943 / JEG-944 storage |
