# **How a DDF Value is made**

A DDF Value is the average of four trade charts, each adjusted to your league.

The four charts are CBS, USA Today, FantasyPros and FantasyCalc. Each one publishes its own number for every player.

**Step 1: Published.** We take each chart's number as it is published.

**Step 2: Indexed.** Each chart uses its own units, so we put them on one scale by multiplying every published number by one factor for that chart (the indexed total divided by the published total).

**Step 3: Adjusted.** We adjust each indexed number for your scoring, your team count and whether your league starts a second quarterback.

**Step 4: Averaged.** The four adjusted numbers are averaged. The result is the DDF Value you see.

**Example.** For Jahmyr Gibbs in a 12-team Half PPR league in week 9, the four adjusted numbers were 99.6, 71.9, 71.2 and 94.4. Their mean is 84.275, shown as 84.3.

Every step is computed when you ask. Open "How we get our numbers" to follow any player's number from the published chart to the final value.
