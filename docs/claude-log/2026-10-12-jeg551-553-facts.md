## What was done

Computed three sets of facts for the research post 1 (JEG-544) rewrite, all at 12-team full PPR on the approved defaults (commit 90983715):

1. **Fill-in rates by bench depth (JEG-551):** RB depth-1 50.9%, depth-2 11.5%, depth-3 0.9%; WR depth-1 46.3%, depth-2 9.2%, depth-3 0.7%; QB 18.7%; TE 21.7%. The old draft had RB 34%/4% and WR 48% from the decision build (bye share 7.2%); the approved defaults (bye 0.0781, m_recent) give significantly higher RB fill-in rates because n_per_team=3 (includes flex).

2. **Week-by-week bench share (JEG-551):** Overall declines from 8.8% (Week 1) to 8.0% (Week 17). The old draft said 8.7% to 7.7%; the approved defaults give a flatter trend. Decline driven by bye share dropping to zero in playoffs and sigma shrinking with horizon.

3. **Bench-slots effect (JEG-553):** Overall bench share barely changes across 4-8 bench slots (8.8% to 9.0%). Zero inversions at all slot counts. Each additional bench player sits deeper where fill-in probability drops geometrically with depth.
