## 2026-10-26 - JEG-1112 Compare fits 1366x768 again (drop the propose/accept lines, pin the guard trades)

Contract: the merge-blocking `v2-render` check was red on main since the automated data rebuild b227f453 changed which trade `tests/test_v2_waterfall_render.py` measures. Jeremy chose option D (2026-10-26, multiple choice): "D: drop the two 'propose / accept' heads (Recommended)". Remove them from the Compare verdict card so the give / receive / verdict block fits the fold at 1366x768 and 1440x900, and harden the guard so a data rebuild cannot move the trade under test.

What the "heads" are: the `#v2CHeads` block, two lines "Should you propose or accept? ... Our value: ..." and "Will they accept? ... Charts agree: ..." (JEG-505, JEG-895). They are answer lines with a level and reasons, not labels for a region, so there was no accessible name to keep: the card keeps `aria-labelledby="v2CVerdictTitle"` and the Deal read keeps its own label. Removing them retires feature-contract line CT-02 (as written by Jeremy 2026-10-09 / 2026-10-22), so CT-02 is rewritten in `docs/feature-contract.md` as the removal, with the decision quoted.

### What changed
- `app/v2/shell.html`, `app/v2/v2.js`, `app/v2/v2.css`: the `#v2CHeads` markup, `renderHeadlines()` and its label tables, the `headlines` field and the `lowConfidence` input of `compareView`, the `.v2-cheads` / `.v2-chead .q|.a|.c|.why` rules and the two phone/short-viewport `.v2-cheads` spacing rules. `.v2-chead` itself stays (it is also the Compare card head). `trade.js tradeHeadlines` and `tests/test_v2_headlines.py` are untouched and now unused by the page.
- `tests/test_v2_waterfall_render.py`: the measured trade is two fixed-key trades (`PINNED_TRADES`), each on a fresh page at every viewport: "lose" (Gibbs + Smith-Njigba for Nacua + Chase, the `you win, but ...` copy with a dissenting source) and "win" (Gibbs + McCaffrey for Walker + Chase, the long `... you lose this trade. A manager who trades off ...` copy that names each dissenting source twice). The gap player is fixed too (`PINNED_GAP`, always simulated). Each trade must take its own branch and copy (`check_verdict(branch=)`), so a rebuild that flips a trade fails with a message instead of silently measuring the other copy. The "verdict names no source" mutation now also removes the second sentence and must be caught on each pinned trade alone; a new mutation drops only the second sentence on the win trade. Nothing was loosened: same viewports, same layout rules, same tolerances.
- Other tests that read the removed lines were updated because the rule they pin was changed by Jeremy (option D), not to go green: `tests/test_v2_compare_render.py` (headline recompute and the mutations that only touched the headline), `tests/test_v2_plain_copy_render.py` (headline copy checks and 3 mutations), `tests/test_v2_compare_empty_side_render.py` (JEG-808 stale-verdict guard reworked onto the verdict text, waterfall and Deal read, with 3 new mutations), `tests/test_v2_contract_render.py` (CT-02 is now a live check that the lines are absent, with a mutation that puts them back). `docs/risk-register.md` row GAP-COMPARE-HEADLINE-UNGUARDED notes which of its guards were retired.
