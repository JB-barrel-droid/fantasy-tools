## 2026-10-08 - GAP-CBS-WEEK5-HOLD: CBS Week 5 held at review (fix/cbs-week5-hold)

Contract: find exactly why rebuild-chain run 37772112376 (11:45Z) held CBS
Week 5 (`native_drift:full_12` fail, `coverage:full_12/TE` fail) and fix the
root cause in the review logic.

### Cause
1. `newer_vintage()` returned None. The chain builds sections without
   `--week-designated`, so the candidate's top-level `week_designated` is
   None. The week is in `source_provenance.week_designated` (int 5, from the
   importer's week column) and `content_vintage` "Week 5". The reviewer read
   only the top-level field, then tried `content_vintage` as a date; "Week 5"
   is not a date. Fixture side was fine (combo vintage "Week 4"; the
   per-combo vintage was NOT a partial-promote artefact). The 10-07 tests
   used a synthetic candidate with top-level `week_designated: 5`, a shape
   the chain never produces.
2. Tail-churn rule: tail = fixture native < 10% of the position top. CBS's
   chart floors at ~5 (Week 4 PPR TE: top 33, last-listed Schultz 5 = 15%),
   so no CBS player could ever be tail. Dropped: Pitts 7, Waller 6, Schultz 5
   (ranks 11-17 of 17, all in the bottom 7% of the 5..33 range). n_priced was
   not stale (fixture set 17 = recorded 17).

### Fix (pipelines/review_comparison_candidate.py only)
- `vintage_week()`: week from top-level `week_designated`, else
  `source_provenance.week_designated`, else exact "Week N" `content_vintage`
  (promote_comparison_section.py's precedence). A date is never read as a week.
- Tail measured within the list's own [floor, top] range. Floor 0 (most
  sources store the tail at 0.0) gives the old rule unchanged. Cap,
  named-drop, still-listed, review-row and live-contradiction rules unchanged.
