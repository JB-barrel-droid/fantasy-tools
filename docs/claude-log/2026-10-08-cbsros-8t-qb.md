## 2026-10-08 - GAP-CBSROS-8T-NO-QB: CBS ROS prices no QB at 8 teams (fix/cbsros-8t-qb)

Contract: find why the CBS ROS series prices no quarterback at 8 teams (every
scoring) and fix the root cause, with browser/server parity.

### Cause
The problem is in the browser only. The data and the chain are fine.
- On the 2026-10-02 CBS ROS pool (players.json `cbsros_ppg`) the 8-team QB tier
  is very flat: QB2-QB13 project 22.0-22.9 ppg, waiver line 19.19, starter line
  22.37. At the 0.15 bench share the solved bench rate is negative (-0.074);
  the feasible window starts at 0.1602.
- The leg builders (`build_ddf_two_tier_leg.py::build_leg`,
  `build_cbsros_ddf_leg.py`) handle a negative bench rate by stepping UP to the
  lowest feasible share (JEG-74). So the chain's CBS ROS section prices all 62
  QBs at full_8/half_8/standard_8.
- `TwoTier.calibratePositionFeasible` (curve-widget.js), which the live CBS ROS,
  Razzball and ESPN paths use, only fell back DOWN (economics break). On a
  negative bench rate it returned invalid, `ddfTwoTierValuesForSource` withheld
  QB, and the engine's cbsros map had no QB at any 8-team setup.
- ESPN and Razzball at 8 teams: 0.15 is feasible for QB (Razzball 8t QB 0.15;
  ESPN window 0.086-0.258 per the QB8 investigation), so they were not affected.

### Fix
- `calibratePositionFeasible` now mirrors the leg builders step for step: on
  "bench rate ... not positive" it steps up 0.01 at a time to the first
  feasible share, then runs 15 bisections toward the window's lower edge. The
  downward branch is unchanged.
- The same change is in `pipelines/twotier_reference.py` (the Python mirror
  used by `check_twotier_parity.py`), plus a new vector
  `calibrate_position_feasible_below_window` (the real 8-team PPR CBS ROS QB
  tier).
- New `tests/test_cbsros_8t_qb.py` (added to `make test-core`) with harness
  `tests/cbsros_live_section_harness.js`.
- `tests/test_below_leg_zero_render.py`: its "no floor" mutation was only ever
  caught by the CBS ROS 8-team QB withholding (no other projected player sits
  above a leg's floor in the current data). With the fix it stopped failing.
  The mutation now also turns the upward step off, which recreates that state.
  A new control checks that the correct rule passes on that same state, so the
  failure comes from the floor rule and not from the withholding. Docstring
  example updated. No assertion was loosened.

### Found, not fixed (logged GAP-CBSROS-LIVE-POOL)
The browser's live CBS ROS values differ from the published section by up to
10.7 points in every league shape. Example: standard_12 Jaxon Smith-Njigba is
50.9 live and 40.2 in the section. Cause: the leg drops 16 suffix-name players
as unresolved_identity (Kenneth Walker III, Michael Penix Jr., Marvin Harrison
Jr., ...). The browser prices them from players.json, which moves the waiver
and starter lines. With the pool restricted to the section's players, the gap
drops to 0.2 or less.
