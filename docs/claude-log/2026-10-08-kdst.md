## 2026-10-08 - K/DST removed from the pipeline (fix/kdst-daily, GAP-029)

Contract: the lane started as "refresh K/DST from ESPN daily behind the default-off
Include K/DST toggle". That toggle no longer exists: commit 0ca6ab4 (JEG-211,
Jeremy 2026-10-03, "honest exclusion") removed it and all K/DST chart surfaces.
The integrator re-asked Jeremy. His answer: keep K/DST off the chart (JEG-211
stands) and stop carrying K/DST at all (option B). This entry covers B only.
A daily ESPN K/DST puller was drafted and then deleted before any commit.

JEG-211 rationale (docs/kdst-source-audit.md, commit 0ca6ab4):
- ESPN is the only K/DST source, so there is no peer to compare or adjust against.
- K+DST are about 2% of the ESPN pie.
- The value-above-waivers curve is nearly flat (top over replacement: K 1.15x, DST 1.26x).
- One scoring-invariant number per player.
- The pull was stale.

Branch pushed. Not merged.
