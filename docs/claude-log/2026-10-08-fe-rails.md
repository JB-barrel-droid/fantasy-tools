## Decisions made without asking

- Chart options offers Top 100 as well as the frame's Full / Top 25 / Top 50, because Top 100 is the
  chart's default window; without it the panel could not show the current state.
- Position shares in Weights & bench are read only (no engine setter, BE-2); the frame's editable
  fields are not faked.
- Starter / Bench / Waiver presets use the engine's roster boundaries from `getZones()` in the ranking
  series' full order, mapped onto the filtered list.
