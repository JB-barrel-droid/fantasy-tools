## 2026-10-16 - JEG-481 second slice: league import and roster sanity (app/v2/finder.js only)

- Done: each roster on Find a trade can be imported from a Sleeper league ID (rosters, users and the 15 MB player directory, fetched on use), a public ESPN league ID, or pasted names. Names are matched to the engine's rows (case, punctuation and Jr/III ignored; a shared name is told apart by position, else left unmatched and listed). A player on the other roster is left out and said so. A private ESPN league (401/403) says it is private and offers pasting.
- Roster sanity: two checkboxes, both on by default (Jeremy not asked): "Keep my starting lineup filled" and "Keep theirs filled". A trade is thin for a side when it leaves fewer players at QB/RB/WR/TE than the league starts and fewer than before, or fewer DDF starter-tier players (the engine's own `ddfTier`) than before, short of the slots. FLEX and SUPERFLEX are not counted. Dropped trades are counted in a note; with the boxes off, a thin trade is listed with "Leaves you/them thin: ...". The typed rosters also get a note for a starting position they do not cover.
