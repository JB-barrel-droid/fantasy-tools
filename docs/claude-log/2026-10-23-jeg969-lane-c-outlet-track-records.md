## 2026-10-23 - JEG-969 Lane C: outlet track records research

Web research into who produces each projection/trade-chart source and what
independent accuracy evidence exists. PR #868 (docs-only, branch
research/JEG-969-outlet-track-records).

### Verified (source named)
- FFA 12-season projection accuracy data (fantasyfootballanalytics.net): ESPN
  MASE .647, CBS .649, FantasyPros .573, simple average .569. Aggregates beat
  every single source every year.
- FFA 11-season DFS accuracy (same site): ESPN QB MAE 6.38, CBS 6.23,
  FantasyPros 6.20. ESPN TE MAE 3.85 (best).
- R-bloggers 5-season study (2012-2016): same ranking pattern.
- FantasyPros 2025 in-season accuracy: Justin Boone (Yahoo) #1. 159+ experts.
- Lane B backtest (our data, 02-aggregator-test-results.md): no aggregator beats
  equal weights; RotoWire and ESPN indistinguishable (paired diff CI includes zero).

### Claimed without independent confirmation
- Razzball (Rudy Gamble) is sole author; no multi-year accuracy study found.
  Absence of evidence is not evidence of poor performance.
- USA Today trade chart appears to be CBS/SportsLine syndication. Could not
  locate a USA Today-authored chart.
- FantasyCalc creator is anonymous; 1.2M+ real trades claimed on product pages.
- RotoWire not separately tracked in FFA studies. Our own data (lane B) is the
  primary evidence for its accuracy.

### Not verified
- Specific FantasyPros accuracy scores for ESPN/CBS/Razzball contributors
  (the accuracy page requires interactive filtering; raw data not fetched).
- Whether USA Today syndication carries any editorial overlay beyond CBS values.
