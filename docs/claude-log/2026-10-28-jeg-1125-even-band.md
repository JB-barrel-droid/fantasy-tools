What the three definitions were on main (951b32be):
- trade.js sideOf (Deal read, Compare): even when |net| < max(1, 0.03 x average side total).

- finder.js pointsAsShown / NET_FLOOR (Find a trade; also trade.js signOf, used by Compare's per-source rows,
  trade story and "Will they accept?"): even when |net| < 0.05, i.e. it shows as 0.0.
