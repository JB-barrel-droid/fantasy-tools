## 2026-10-23 - A11y batch 1 (JEG-896, JEG-898, JEG-902)

Branch `fe/a11y-batch1`, PR open, not merged. Source: live a11y audit 2026-10-22.

### What changed
- JEG-896: Customize tabs (`app/v2/v2.js`) now handle ArrowLeft/ArrowRight (wrapping), Home and End; focus and
  selection move together, roving tabindex kept.
- JEG-898: `syncTitle()` sets `document.title` to `<section> · <view> · Data Driven Football`; an open modal
  (Customize/other panel, player drawer, expanded view) is prefixed. Called from `applyRoute` and a
  MutationObserver on the `hidden` attribute of `#v2Popover`, `#v2Drawer`, `#v2Expand`.
- JEG-902 (`app/v2/v2.css`): new tokens `--v2-field-border` (light #80908B, 3.1:1; dark #62756F, 3.7:1) on
  `.v2-btn`, `.v2-btn-adjust`, selects and search inputs; `--v2-action-text` (dark #38B386) for rank cells
  (4.06 -> 4.6:1); Y-axis toggle text uses `--v2-action-strong`; nav focus ring white.
- Not changed: `.v2-card` borders (containers, not controls), tap sizes (JEG-903).

### Verified
- `tests/test_v2_a11y_batch1_render.py`: keyboard reach of the blend tab, title per route and with Customize
  open, computed-style contrast (rank cell, Y-axis toggle, borders, nav ring) in light and dark. Each of five
  checks is run against a reverted build and must fail.
