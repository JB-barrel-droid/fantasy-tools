### The review fix (a quality review before merge, 2026-10-26)

The review found that the 0-100 scale was only enforced where the reader
types: an absolute value above 100 that arrived from outside the editor (a
share link, an import, a saved record) was applied as it was, and an
adjustment past the scale could be dropped by the packed share link.

Fixed at the doors, in all three engines and in the app:

- `ValueModel.normalizeOverride` (app/trade-value-chart/assets/value-model.js)
  caps an absolute value at 100 and an adjustment at the span of the scale.
- `_override_spec` (pipelines/value_reference.py and
  pipelines/spec_reference/value_pipeline.py) does the same, so the three
  engines still agree.
- `app/v2/overrides.js` puts every override that arrives from outside the
  editor through one `normalizeEntry`: the saved record (`readOverride`), the
  editor (`setOverride`), an import (`importDoc`), a link
  (`parseOvParam`, the packed `z:` and `z2:` forms) and the link's session
  state. The packed form no longer drops an entry: its tenths are clamped to
  the two bytes they travel in.
- `docs/v2-design-notes.md` — the row fields were described the wrong way
  round: `valueOverride` is the effective value the reader sees,
  `valueOverrideSpec` is what the reader set.
- `app/values-explainer/reviewed.json` — four of the five files it hashes
  changed, so the review was re-marked: `make values-explainer MARK_REVIEWED=1`
  prints "logic changed since review: none".

The new tests fail without the fix (checked by running them against the
pre-fix files: 4 failures, the rest of the suite green):

- `tests.test_value_overrides.test_an_override_from_outside_the_editor_is_capped_at_the_scale`
  — an absolute value of 120 gives 100, an adjustment of 5000 is stored as a
  delta of 100.
- `tests.test_basis_overrides.AdjustmentOverrides.test_an_override_from_outside_the_editor_is_capped_at_the_scale`
  — the same cap in all three engines.
- `tests.test_v2_override_adjustments_render` — a link that carries
  `ov=key:120` and a file that imports a value of 120 both reach the reader
  as 100.
