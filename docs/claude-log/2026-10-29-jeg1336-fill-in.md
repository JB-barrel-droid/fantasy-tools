and how far DDF Value moves when it does, find why, and lay out rule options
with before/after DDF Value examples. Research only: no value math, defaults,
ranking, Supabase or deploy.

- The hooked `run_pipeline` (rebuilt from its source with the fill-set and
  estimate lines as hooks) equals `value_reference.run_pipeline` on all 14
  settings under option A: `check_equal` in `tools/research/jeg1336_fill_in.py`
  runs first and stops otherwise (it passed).

  intercept 4.58 (its last 10 listed RBs are all 5 or 6), so Raheim Sanders
  (1.51 PPG, no chart lists him) gets 4.73 against CBS's 0.32 waiver line:
  read from `sources.cbs.positions.RB.imputation_ratios` of a reference run.

  allocation held): fill set changed in 536 of 1,372; membership-only steps
  over 1.0 in 56 under A, 9 under B, 17 under C, 1 under D, 0 under B+D.
- Sweep (3,716 one-source ±0.5 and ±1 PPG cases near a fill cutoff,
  allocation held): 23 cases over 1.0 under A (all players no chart lists:
  Sanders, Brooks, Juszczyk); 0 under D; the remaining ~1.0 under B, C and

- One-time change under D: only players with a curve-path estimate drop
