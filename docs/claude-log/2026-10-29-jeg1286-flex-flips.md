Contract: measure how often one player at the flex or bench cutoff reprices a
position, across the 12-setting grid and the two real leagues' settings, and
lay out rule options with before/after DDF Value examples. Research only:
no value math, defaults, ranking, Supabase or deploy.

  on all 14 settings: `check_equal` in `tools/research/jeg1286_flex_flips.py`
  runs first and stops the run otherwise (it passed).

  comparisons, median 1.73 DDF Value points from the flip (free vs the earlier
  bake's allocation held), worst 5.58.

  allocation; 220 of the 239 cases over 1.0 are flips; the other 19 are the
  VP-2.4 fill-in step (Chris Brooks standard/14, Raheim Sanders standard/12),
  checked by reading their rows (CBS lists neither; CBS estimate 6.97 and 5.51).
- Option B (fractional seats, σ 0.75) is continuous: a 0.1 PPG scan of
  Coker's CBS ROS projection (standard/10) moves Gibbs about 0.03 per step
  under B against a 1.83 jump under A at -1.0 PPG.
