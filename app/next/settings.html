Superflex
ⓘSuperflex means one extra starting spot that can be filled by a QB, RB, WR or TE. Most teams put a second quarterback there because QBs score the most, so quarterbacks become worth more. It is not the same as a 2-QB league, which requires you to start two quarterbacks. If your league has a 2-QB slot, set QB to 2 in your lineup and leave Superflex off. If it has a flex spot that lists QB as eligible, turn Superflex on.
