                ⓘTwo kinds of source. Analyst charts: CBS Sports, USA Today and FantasyPros publish analysts' trade values. Live market: FantasyCalc reflects live market activity, what people actually pay in trades. Each chart uses its own published numbers, not adjusted to your league. The comparison is a percentage (what they receive minus what they send, divided by what they send), so charts with different scales are comparable. "Helps your pitch" means they receive more than they send by more than 1%, so the chart supports the message; "Works against you" means they send more by more than 1%, and the message then makes no chart claim. Up to 25% the label shows the exact percentage; above 25% it says "a lot more", and the exact figure appears under the picker when you choose that chart.
