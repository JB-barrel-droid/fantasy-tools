      ⓘ Sources & methodology — Compare source definitions, normalization and depth assumptions.

      CBS Sports is an analyst chart (analysts' published trade values). FantasyCalc is the live market: live market activity, what people actually pay in trades. Both series are indexed for comparison with DDF Value. Indexed values differ from each chart's published numbers. DDF Value is the average of the four trade charts' adjusted values (CBS Sports, USA Today, FantasyPros and FantasyCalc) at your league settings. A source that does not list a player shows —, never 0.

      On Buy / Sell, the Charts value averages the charts that list the player, with a count out of four next to it (4/4 means all four charts list him). Values a chart does not publish are left out, not estimated. In the player card, the gap is DDF Value minus the average of the CBS and FantasyCalc indexed values.
