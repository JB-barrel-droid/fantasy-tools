      CBS Sports is an analyst chart (analysts' published trade values). FantasyCalc is the live market: live market activity, what people actually pay in trades. Both series are indexed for comparison with DDF Value. Indexed values differ from each chart's published numbers. Historical values use Half PPR, 12 teams and modeled depth.

      On Buy / Sell, the Charts value averages the charts that list the player, with a count out of four next to it: 3/4 rows use the three analyst charts (CBS Sports, FantasyPros and USA Today), 2/4 rows the CBS Sports and FantasyCalc indexed values. In the player card, the gap is DDF Value minus the average of the CBS and FantasyCalc indexed values.
