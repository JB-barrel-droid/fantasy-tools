      Glossary
      
        
      The terms this site uses for its numbers. Each term name is a link you can copy.
      
        
      
          
      Published
      A trade chart’s own number for a player, in that chart’s own units.
      
          
      Indexed
      Published × one factor for the chart, so all four charts sit on one scale. The factor is the total DDF Value of the players the chart lists ÷ their total published value; filled-in players are left out of both totals. Source build-up shows the two totals beside each chart’s factor. One factor per chart means indexing never changes that chart’s player order. Adjusted values do not depend on a chart’s scale, so indexing changes no DDF number: it only lets you read each chart on DDF Value’s scale.
      
          
      Adjusted
      The indexed value fitted to your league settings: each chart’s value above waivers, weighted at starter or bench level by where the player ranks at his position for your league’s roster sizes and team count, then rescaled so each chart’s total equals the league’s value pie. The league’s starter, bench and waiver counts and each position’s share of the pie come from all the included sources, the projections too (held ones included): the counts from the projections’ average points per game, the shares from the average over every included source. DDF Value then averages only the four charts’ adjusted values.
      
          
      Value pie
      The fixed total that every source’s adjusted values add up to: 28 per starting slot × teams × starting slots, so 2,352 at the default of 12 teams and 7 starting slots. Each position gets its share of the pie, split between its starters and its bench. The Value pie tab shows something smaller: how one player’s DDF Value splits across the four charts.
      
          
      Waiver line
      For each source and position, about what a free pickup is worth: for a trade chart, the value of the first player past the league’s rostered count; for a projection, the average of the 7 players around that seat (averaged again when the league’s seats split more than one way). A player’s value above waivers is his value minus that line, never below 0, and a player at or below it adjusts to 0.
      
          
      DDF Value
      The mean of the four adjusted charts: the three analyst charts (CBS Sports, USA Today, FantasyPros) and the live market (FantasyCalc). A player your league leaves on the waiver wire is worth about 0.
      
          
      DDF (Projection based)
      An alternate version of DDF Value built from projected points (ESPN, CBS rest of season, Razzball) instead of trade charts.
      
          
      Analyst charts
      The average of the CBS Sports, USA Today and FantasyPros indexed values (each chart’s published numbers on DDF Value’s common scale), with no adjustment for your league. It shows only when all three charts run this week and each gives the player a value. These three are analysts’ trade charts: opinions of what players are worth, not trades. Trade & Waiver Recommendations and Build a Trade use it as the default Their valuation.
      
          
      Live market
      FantasyCalc, shown as Live market (FantasyCalc): live market activity, what people actually pay in trades. It is one of the four charts in DDF Value and is never averaged into Analyst charts.
      
          
      Starter / Bench / Waiver
      A league-level role, given the league’s roster sizes and team count: the league has a set number of starter, bench and waiver places at each position. Starters are the players good enough to fill the league’s starting slots, bench players are the next ones expected to sit on benches, and waiver players are the available players left over. It does not depend on whose team he is on or on your own lineup. DDF Value values starters at starter level and bench players at bench level. Source build-up’s Role column is each source’s own ranking against those starter and roster counts, so a player’s role can differ from one chart to another.
      
          
      Override
      Your own value for a player. It replaces DDF Value on every tab, is applied after DDF’s numbers, and leaves the analyst charts and the live market unchanged. Add one on Values.
      
          
      Basis
      Which sources DDF Value is built from. The default is the four trade charts, weighted equally; the numbers on this page use it.
      
          
      Value above waivers
      How much better a player is than the best one you could pick up for free at his position, counting all his points: each trade chart’s value minus that chart’s waiver line at the position (never below 0), on the same value-pie scale as DDF Value, averaged over the four charts. It comes before DDF’s starter and bench weighting. DDF Value starts from this and adjusts it for where he ranks in your league: players good enough to start at their position, given roster sizes and team count, are valued at starter level, and players expected to sit on benches at bench level. So top starters have a higher DDF Value than value above waivers, and bench players a lower one.
      

          
      Missing (—)
      A dash means a source has no value there: it is unavailable, never zero. A chart that is held this week is left out of DDF Value, not counted as zero. A chart that does not list a player can still adjust him to 0 (at or below its waiver line), and that 0 counts. The same holds for indexed values: a player past the end of a chart’s list shows 0 there. A player the engine filled in from the other charts shows a dash on Values and Buy / Sell, because an estimate is not that chart’s own number; these Learn pages show the estimate, marked est.
      
          
      Zero
      A value of zero is real: the player sits at or below that source’s waiver line for your league.
