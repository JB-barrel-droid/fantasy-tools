Use DDF in your AI assistant

Add Data Driven Football to the AI assistant you already use, and ask it about your own league in plain words. It prices your players and trades with DDF values set for your league, then sends you back to the site to explore, compare and decide. Connecting is one step, with no sign-in.

Take DDF into your AI chat

Copy a ready-to-paste prompt for your preferred AI chatbot. It walks you through connecting the tool, then your league.

On a phone you get a shorter version of the prompt.

Connect the tool

DDF is a free remote MCP server. Add this address to your assistant as a custom connector, with authentication set to none. Menu names change between app updates; if yours differ from the steps below, open the app's own help page on adding a custom MCP connector.

ChatGPT

Once connected, the assistant tests the connection with one call. If it fails, it tells you what failed. Refresh or start a new chat and try once more. The DDF prompt never asks for passwords, cookies or tokens.

What your assistant can do

Ask in plain words, for example which of two players is worth more in a 10-team half-PPR superflex league. The assistant calls these tools with your league settings.

  • get_valuesRanked players with DDF value and tier for your league, filtered by position if you like.
  • get_player_valueValue and tier for the players you name, with a note when a name could mean more than one player.
  • trade_targetsPlayers close in value to one you name, within a range you set, so you can see who is a fair ask.
  • compare_tradeAdds up both sides of a trade and gives a verdict, or shows each trade chart's give, receive and net.
  • find_tradesLooks for trades between your roster and one other team, with a fit label and checks on whether it works for both sides.
  • find_trades_leagueRanks trades across your leaguemates' rosters, up to 15 teams, and builds a package.
  • risers_fallersWho moved most in value since the previous update, and where the sources disagree.
  • audit_valuesHealth checks on the values, and how each source builds up to the DDF number.
  • explainPlain-language explanations of how DDF values and the site's tools work.
  • make_statePacks your league settings, rosters, a trade or a target into a link to the site, plus a block you can paste.
  • read_stateReads a link or DDF block you bring back from the site and picks up where you left off.
  • site_next_stepsSuggests two or three things to try on the site for where you are now.

From chat to the site

The chat is for reading your league and quick answers. The site is where you explore, compare and decide. After your league settings and rosters are in, the assistant gives you a link that opens the site already set to your league, and gives you a fresh one after each later step.

The link holds your league

Your league settings (scoring, team count, superflex, roster slots), rosters, and the trade or player you were working on are packed into the link itself, after the #. Nothing is stored on a server, and that part of a link is never sent to one.

The link ends with a short checksum, so the site can tell if a chat app garbled it.

Links open the page that fits: team assessment, recommendations, Build a Trade, values or waivers. Safari older than 16.4 cannot read them.

Link got mangled? Paste it

Long links are sometimes cut or re-wrapped by chat apps, so the assistant also gives you a DDF block. Paste either one on the site and it picks up where you left off, with your league loaded.

Going the other way, a link or block you bring back from the site is read by the assistant, which continues from your changes.

No tools? Paste mode

If your assistant cannot add MCP tools, you can still use it for the tedious part. It will not make up values.

  1. Paste the DDF prompt into the chat. It collects your league settings and every roster, and tidies messy copied text into one team per block, one player per line.
  2. Open the site's Connect page and paste that clean list. The site prices everything for your league.

You will not get live pricing inside the chat, since that needs the tools. Where a tool-enabled app is available, the chat can do more.
