The same method, starting from rest-of-season projections. Check a trade also shows the points per week and for the rest of the season. On this preview the value-scale figure is one projection source. Show the math opens each point read.

The Market What the trade charts say before our adjustments — roughly how the other manager will see it.

The other team's read: the trade charts before our adjustments. On this preview that is the chart average, from their side.

Buy, Sell, Fair price, Change

Buy means DDF Projected Value is ahead of the Market. Sell means the Market is ahead of DDF Projected Value. Fair price means the two are close, and those names stay in the player picker. Change is how a number moved. This preview is one day, so Change is not on the cards.
