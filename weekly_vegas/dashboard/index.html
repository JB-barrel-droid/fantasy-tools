How to read thisWhat Vegas, ECR, ESPN, and the fantasy reads actually mean.

Vegas. Fantasy points implied by this week’s player prop numbers. PropLine is the primary leg: raw DraftKings, FanDuel, BetMGM, and Pinnacle props are translated locally, never taken from a vendor’s blended number. Five uncovered players carry a separately labeled FDS fantasy projection fallback.

ECR (expert consensus). The position-specific ECR projection for the same week and scoring format. It tends to move more slowly and reflects consensus expectations and news.

ESPN. Mike Clay’s weekly statistical model. It is the tiebreaker: use it to see whether a third method leans closer to Vegas or ECR.

Vegas higher means the sportsbook props imply more weekly fantasy production; ECR higher means the consensus projects more. That direction is a fantasy read, not an over/under recommendation.

Driving stat. Each flagged card names the stat contributing most to the gap. A flag becomes a verified play only with a fresh two-sided sportsbook line and price that clears the play gate.

What a flag is not. It is not a bet recommendation or a start/sit order. All current flags are fantasy reads; there are 0 verified positive-EV plays in this snapshot. Use the raw Vegas and ECR points as the checkable facts.

Usual Vegas tilt. The flagging test first accounts for how far below ECR Vegas normally prices each position this week, so a flag marks a larger-than-usual disagreement rather than the everyday gap.
