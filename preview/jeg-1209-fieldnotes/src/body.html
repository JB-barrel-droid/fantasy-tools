JEG-1209 · review note

Current flow vs proposed

What changes for a reader of Find a trade (whole league), and why. The proposed column is what this preview runs.

Today on the live page

  1. Your league card

    Paste and team list in one long card; the loaded state is implied by the list.

  2. Team summary

    Optional summary columns for either team.

  3. Trade opportunities

    Every opponent is searched. Columns in a fixed order led by package capture. Fit labels per side.

  4. Row details

    Open below the row.

Proposed in revision 3

  1. League status

    One truthful line: teams, players, your team and whether it is saved on this device. Folds away once checked (JEG-1233).

  2. Explore rosters

    From revision 2: both teams side by side, every team discoverable.

  3. Trade preferences

    Package size and minimum backups, with shared preferences named. A change marks results out of date instead of pretending they reran.

  4. Managers, then Trade ideas

    Your read of each manager, then ideas ranked by your gain with Likely or Possible, and long shots in their own Stretch offers section (JEG-1217).

  5. Trade opportunities

    Outcomes first: your starters, then theirs, then package capture. Columns move with buttons (JEG-1232); Filters choose opponents by stable team id with Select all and Clear (JEG-1231).

  6. Inline details

    Open under the row or inside the phone card; heading takes focus; Escape returns to the row.

Why this order

The live table leads with package capture, the number that can rise while your starting lineup falls. In the historical example the top row captures +13.7 while your DDF starters fall 13.7. Showing starters first makes that visible before anyone acts on it.

A workspace, not a wizard: every stage stays one click away and keeps its state, so a returning reader can go straight to opportunities.
