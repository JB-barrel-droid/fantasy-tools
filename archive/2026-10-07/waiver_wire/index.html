    A swap appears at a gain of 2 or more. Each pickup and drop is used once, and thin positions stay protected.

    Value scale: Current value · Week unavailable · Half PPR · 12 teams · base value. Current value · Week unavailable and the frozen pre-week prior use the same starter-weighted 0–70 scale. The board is driven by the direction and destination of that value change.

    How Current value · Week unavailable works: It reprices the latest reassessed per-game projection through the chart’s own starter-weighted model, with the 70-point scale pinned to the frozen pre-week prior.

    What gets credit: Role and expected points carry fully. Scoring above or below expected gets one-tenth credit; the 2024–2025 data shows just 0.01 week-to-week efficiency correlation, so touchdown luck does not repeat. Players whose games have not been played keep their prior unchanged — they are never treated as observed.

    Source dates: every input's snapshot date and live freshness is in the Data sources panel at the top of the page. The retired expert-only view and the older K/DST projection table are no longer shown.

    How the board ranks: Five flat groups, no subcategories. Hot pickups covers the most-added players since Sunday, ranks them by current value, and gives each an ADD or PASS verdict from the value layer. Other pickups are under-the-radar entrants the data likes. Players who stayed rosterable have their own section, including low-owned wire checks and notable value risers. Hold includes players who fell from 10-plus to 1–9, plus anyone displaced from the finite rosterable set who still has positive value. Drop is reserved for players who fell to 0.
